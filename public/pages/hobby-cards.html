Hobby Cards

I first found out about this type of project / hobby from a youtube video I found. The video was discussing about knowing that to make next in terms of your hobbies; when you have so many. It was intriguing to me since I'm inconsistent when it comes to deciding what to do with my free time. This is especially useful because I have so many interests. My version of the hobby cards will be digital because one, I like the portability of it, and two, I don't have as much scrap booking resources available to me at the momment. Regardless, here I have made a place for my hobby cards to be displayed. I will be adding posts below for each peice of artwork I make using the cards. I will also have a list of pending cards I'll need to make in the future.
